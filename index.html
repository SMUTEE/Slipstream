Slipstream

A browser chord instrument. Seven scale degrees, a twelve-detent knob, and notes that slide between chords instead of restarting.

▸ Play
What it does
  • Seven pads are scale degrees, so they keep their meaning in all twelve keys.
  • One knob, twelve detents of harmonic colour, reachable by drag, thumb slide or keyboard.
  • Slide is real portamento — hold a chord, change the key, and it glides.
  • Record four loop layers, each keeping its own synth voice.
  • Arrangements save and share as a short code, and stay transposable for whoever opens them.
No dependencies, no build step. Audio starts on your first tap — browsers require a gesture before a page may make sound. Source
