Slipstream Mobile

The desktop layout works because your eye and your cursor are in different places. On a phone they are the same place, your thumb, and that breaks three things at once.

You cannot record and play at onceThe transport sits around 900px below the pads. Starting a take means scrolling away from the instrument.
Feedback is 300px from your thumbPress and slide changes the chord, but the only readout is a knob at the top of the screen, often behind your hand.
The knob takes 40% of the screen to do nothingThe gesture starts on the pad, so on a phone you never touch the knob. It is a readout wearing a control's clothes.

The moves

Four changes, one of which matters most

Ring under the thumb
The one that actually matters. Mobile is the only place where the person choosing the chord colour cannot see the thing displaying it. Everything else here is layout; this is the interaction being wrong.
Pinned transport
Record, play, layer dots, countdown and setup in a bar that never scrolls, inside the thumb arc, padded for the home indicator.
Knob at 56%
It is a readout on a phone, not a control. Giving back that height is what lets the pads grow and the transport stay pinned.
Setup sheet
Sixteen controls leave the main screen. The grouping is the one already built for desktop, so nothing new has to be invented.
Hide the key map
A nine-key cluster diagram on a device with no keyboard. Below 640px it should not render at all.

Deliberately not proposing

Three things I left alone

Landscape
A wide pad row with the knob beside it is the better instrument, but it collapses the vertical space the sheet needs and doubles the layout work. Worth doing after the portrait layout proves out, not instead of it.
A separate mobile build
All of this is CSS and one overlay inside the existing page. A second codebase for phones would cost more than it returns at this size.
Bigger changes to the gesture
Press and slide is right and does not need replacing. It needs feedback where the hand is, which is a rendering change, not an interaction change.

What I need from you

The ring under the thumb is the one I would build first and the one I am most confident about. The knob shrinking to 56% is the call I am least sure of, because it is the product's face and this makes it smaller. If that trade feels wrong, the alternative is keeping the knob large and letting the pads stay where they are, which keeps the transport pinned but loses the growth in pad size.
