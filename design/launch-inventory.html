0 of 0 done

Two gates. Nothing else matters until these pass.

1. Play it on your own iPhone. Every recording below sends people to a phone browser. The audio bug is fixed and you confirmed sound works, so this is now about feel: is the first note late, does the thumb ring track your finger, do the pads feel big enough. If something is wrong, you want to find it before you have an audience, not during.

2. Make the three seed loops first. You cannot run a relay you have not run yourself, and the loops are props in three of the four videos. Everything downstream depends on having them saved.

Recording 1 of 4

Desktop hero, 45 seconds, 16:9

The master take. The X and LinkedIn videos are cuts of this, and most of your still frames come out of it. No voiceover, text added in post.

0:003 SECCold open on sound. A chord already ringing when the video starts. Start recording with pad 1 already held. Release at 0:03. Text in post: "no app. no signup."
0:036 SECThree chords, nothing else moving. Tap pad 1, pad 4, pad 5. About one second apart. Let each ring. Keep the cursor still between taps. Mouse drift is the most common thing that makes a take unusable.
0:097 SECThe turn. This is the shot. Press and hold pad 1. Without releasing, drag right to E, then down-right to SE. Readout goes C, Cmaj7, C7. Release. The ring forms under the cursor and lights the option you land on. Drag slowly, roughly one second per move, so it is readable.
0:167 SECChange the key while it rings. The beat nobody expects. Turn Hold on. Tap pad 1 and release; the chord sustains. Now change Key from C to E with the mouse. Listen to it glide. Press pad 1 again to stop. Turn Hold off. Hold is necessary here: one pointer cannot both hold a pad and operate the dropdown. Do not try to do it with the keyboard, the dropdown steals focus.
0:239 SECRecord a layer. Key back to C. Turn Click on. Hit record. Play pads 1, 5, 6, 4, one per bar, every 2.5 seconds. The red ring fills and the take ends itself at 10 seconds. Click on gives you the timing and reads as "recording" on camera. You will turn it off before the final playback.
0:328 SECStack a bass layer. The point of layers in one move. Change Voice to Sub. Hit record again. Play pad 1 and pad 5, two bars each. Let it auto-end. Both layers now loop together, each with its own voice. That is the thing to let land.
0:405 SECThe payoff. Turn Click off. Let the full loop play two bars clean. Hit Share, click Copy. Text in post: "this link is the song." Hold the last frame three seconds.
Recording 2 of 4

Vertical hero, 30 seconds, 9:16

Shoot this on the phone, not as a crop of the desktop take. A 16:9 crop cuts off pads 1 and 7, and the phone has the one thing desktop does not: the ring under your thumb.

0:004 SECThumb on a pad, chord rings. Start recording, then press pad 1 with your thumb and hold.
0:048 SECSlide. The ring forms under your thumb. This is the vertical hero shot and it does not exist on desktop. Without lifting, slide right, then down-right. Hold each a beat so the lit label is readable.
0:126 SECThree chords in the thumb arc. Tap 1, 5, 6. Keep the phone steady, do not reframe.
0:1812 SECRecord a loop without leaving the screen. Hit the red button in the pinned bar. Play four chords. Watch the counter run down and the ring close. The point being made here is that you never scrolled. Do not open the setup sheet during this take.
Recording 3 of 4

The explainer, 30 seconds, 9:16

Talking. The highest-reaching of the four, because teaching content travels past people who would never search for a chord tool.

"Chords aren't hard. The names are hard.

Nobody says 'play B minor seven flat five'. What they mean is 'play the seventh one'.

So I made the buttons the numbers. [play 1, 4, 5, 6]

That's a progression. I never said a chord name and neither did you.

Change the key. [change to E] Same buttons. Still works.

Free, in your browser. Link in bio."

Things that ruin a take

The shortest useful version

If you only get one evening: play it on your phone, make one seed loop, shoot the vertical hero, and cut the seven second hook out of it. That is enough to post on TikTok and Instagram, which is where the first traffic comes from. The desktop take and the carousel can follow a few days later without hurting anything.
